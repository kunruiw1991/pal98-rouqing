🗺️ Chapter 1 English Quick Walkthrough

1. Li Xiaoyao's Inn (Yuhang Town):
Press Space/Enter after Aunt Li wakes you with a frying pan. Search your bedroom for Wooden Shoes and Short Coat. Go downstairs and talk to the Drunken Taoist at the door.

2. Kitchen & Wine Jar:
Enter the kitchen, grab the Luzhou Old Cellar Wine and Pan-Fried Dumplings from the table, and give the wine to the Drunken Taoist — he invites you to Ten-Mile Slope (Shili Po) at midnight to learn swordplay!

3. Fairy Island (Xianling Island):
When Aunt Li collapses, get the Guiyuan Pill from Dr. Wang next door, then sail with Zhang Si from the pier to Fairy Island. Smash the 6 elemental statues on the lotus pond using the stone tablet hammer to open the path to Qianye Zen Palace and meet Zhao Ling'er!
